Executive Officers and Associates

Roles & Responsibilities

Executive Officers lead the organization’s day-to-day administration and operations, implementing approved programs, policies, and decisions within their respective areas of responsibility. Executive Associates work alongside them by providing administrative, technical, program, and operational support. Together, they help ensure that Himig Hiraya operates effectively, responsibly, and in accordance with its mission, values, By-Laws, and organizational goals.
