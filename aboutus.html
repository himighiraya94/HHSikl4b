Himig Hiraya

Himig Hiraya signifies a hymn born from hope, dreams, and aspirations “Himig bunga ng pag-asa, pangarap, at hangarin.”


More than a name, it reflects the heart of who we are: voices united by a shared hope, inspired by meaningful dreams, and driven by the aspiration to create a lasting difference through music, service, and community.

MISSION

Guided by faith-inspired values, Himig Hiraya seeks to share God's love through music, compassionate service, and meaningful community engagement.

The organization is committed to helping individuals and communities, promoting kindness and responsible care toward animals, and fostering hope, unity, leadership, and personal growth through faith, service, and collaboration.

VISION

To be a trusted, sustainable, and faith-inspired organization that transforms lives and strengthens communities through music, compassionate service, leadership development, and meaningful partnerships, while promoting responsible care for people and animals and creating lasting impact for generations to come.

PURPOSE

MUSIC AND FAITH

To use music to inspire faith, hope, compassion, unity, and positive action in communities, churches, and other institutions.

COMMUNITY SERVICE AND ADVOCACY

To undertake charitable, educational, environmental, faith-based, and community initiatives that promote social responsibility, inclusivity, and the well-being of people and communities.

ANIMAL WELFARE

To promote kindness, responsible care, rescue, and humane treatment of stray, vulnerable, and companion animals through advocacy and community action.

MEMBER AND LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT

To provide opportunities for members to develop their talents, character, leadership, spiritual growth, and commitment to volunteerism through service, fellowship, and learning.

PARTNERSHIPS AND COLLABORATION

To foster unity among members and build meaningful partnerships with churches, communities, government agencies, organizations, and individuals that share or support the purposes of Himig Hiraya.

SUSTAINABILITY AND SOCIAL ENTERPRISE

To develop sustainable initiatives, social enterprises, income-generating activities, and partnerships that strengthen the organization’s capacity to fulfill its mission, expand its impact, create meaningful opportunities, and serve future generations.

CORE VALUES

FAITH

We place God at the center of our purpose and serve with humility, gratitude, and love.

KINDNESS

We treat people and all living creatures with respect, empathy, and dignity.

COMPASSION

We respond to the needs of people, communities, and animals with mercy, generosity, and genuine care.

SERVICE

We dedicate our time, talents, and resources to serving God, our communities, and those in need.

INTEGRITY

We act with honesty, accountability, transparency, and ethical responsibility.

UNITY

We foster fellowship, inclusivity, mutual respect, and collaboration toward a shared purpose.

EXCELLENCE

We pursue quality, professionalism, discipline, and continuous improvement in all that we do.

STEWARDSHIP

We responsibly care for the resources, relationships, opportunities, and creation entrusted to us.

LEADERSHIP

We develop servant leaders who lead with character, humility, responsibility, and positive example.

HOPE

We inspire resilience, possibility, and a better future through faith, music, and service.
