Founding Leadership Council

Roles & Responsibilities

The Founding Leadership Council brings together the Founder and the original senior leaders who helped establish, shape, and develop Himig Hiraya from its early beginnings. Together, they serve as stewards of the organization’s mission, vision, values, identity, and founding principles while providing strategic guidance, institutional knowledge, leadership continuity, and long-term direction. Their enduring role helps ensure that Himig Hiraya remains true to its purpose while continuing to grow, adapt, and build a sustainable legacy for future generations.
