Functional Leadership Assignments

Roles & Responsibilities

Functional Leadership supports the long-term growth and sustainability of Himig Hiraya by entrusting qualified members with key areas based on their experience, expertise, and contributions. These leaders provide guidance, continuity, coordination, and support across areas such as governance, finance, programs, partnerships, communications, music and artistic development, multimedia, community service, and animal welfare—helping strengthen the organization and advance its mission for generations to come.
